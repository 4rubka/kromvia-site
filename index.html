A Minecraft launcher with its own client built in.

Kromvia runs Minecraft: Java Edition with a built-in client mod: HUD modules, zoom, cosmetics, and a custom menu. You also get instances, mods and servers in one place.

Features

One app for playing, without setting up loaders and mods by hand.

Built-in client

Ships with a lightweight client mod: HUD modules, zoom, fog and sky tweaks, a custom main menu. One build for each supported Minecraft version.

Instances

Separate builds for each version and loader (Fabric, Forge, NeoForge, Quilt), each with its own icon, mods and settings. Prism Launcher instances can be imported.

Mods & packs

Search for mods, resource packs and shaders and install them into any instance, then turn them on or off without opening the game folder.

Servers

A quick-play list of servers you can join in one click, straight from the home screen.

Cosmetics

A wardrobe with 3D cosmetics previewed on your skin in the launcher. Full in-game support is coming.

15 languages

English, Ukrainian and 13 more languages, with dark and light themes.

Sign-in & privacy

You play with your own Microsoft account. The launcher only lets accounts that own Minecraft: Java Edition launch the game. Offline or cracked accounts are not supported.

  1. Microsoft sign-in. You sign in on Microsoft's own page using a device code. Kromvia never sees your password.
  2. Xbox Live and Minecraft services. The launcher exchanges that sign-in for a Minecraft token and checks that you own the game and have a profile.
  3. Stored on your computer only. Tokens are saved locally on your computer and are never sent to Kromvia servers. Sign out at any time to remove them.
